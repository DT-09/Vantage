# Vantage — initial commercial offer

## Positioning

A read-only invoice-to-payment exception review for B2B SaaS finance teams.

**Buyer:** Founder, controller, finance lead, or revenue-operations owner at a small B2B SaaS company that uses invoice exports and a payment processor export.

**Trigger:** Month-end close, unexplained paid-invoice balances, an accounting handoff, a billing migration, or a finance-team review that currently relies on manual spreadsheet matching.

## Deliverable

The customer supplies two appropriately redacted CSV exports using the documented Vantage schema:

1. Invoice export.
2. Payment export.

Vantage returns:

- A machine-generated exception CSV.
- A summary JSON with record counts and exception categories.
- A short human-readable review note that distinguishes observed mismatches from hypotheses.
- Source identifiers and arithmetic for each exception.
- A list of items the customer's finance owner should verify.

The review does not change customer records or contact customers.

## Pilot price and time cap

**$300 fixed fee, maximum two hours of delivery work.**

This equals $150 per delivery hour if completed within the cap. If the work cannot be completed within the cap, stop and obtain written approval for a revised scope. Do not silently exceed the cap.

The price is a proposed test, not evidence that buyers have agreed to pay it. Do not claim validated demand until a real buyer accepts the scope and pays.

## Recurring offer

**$500 per monthly review**, with scope and data volume agreed in writing. Recurrence is offered only after the pilot demonstrates a repeatable need and the customer requests it.

## Qualification — accept only when

- The company has a real invoice/payment reconciliation workflow.
- The finance owner can explain the current process and its cost.
- The supplied data can be safely minimized and redacted.
- The requested review fits the supported CSV schema.
- The buyer accepts the limitations and review responsibility.
- The contracting and payment arrangements are handled by an authorized adult while the founder is a minor.

## Explicit exclusions

No accounting, tax, audit, legal, investment, or revenue-recognition advice. No claim of confirmed revenue leakage, recovered cash, compliance, or guaranteed savings. No production credentials, API access, direct database access, payment initiation, collections, record mutation, or customer communications. No unsupported currency or billing-system formats.

## Acceptance criteria

A paid pilot is complete only when the customer receives the agreed files, the record counts reconcile to the supplied exports, all generated exceptions include source identifiers or an explicit reason they do not, and the customer confirms receipt.

## Demand validation

Track:
- Qualified buyer conversations.
- Buyers who share a real workflow description.
- Written pilot acceptances.
- Paid pilots.
- Delivery hours per pilot.
- Repeat purchases.
- Exception usefulness confirmed by the customer's finance owner.

Website visits, GitHub activity, demo downloads, and compliments are not revenue validation.
